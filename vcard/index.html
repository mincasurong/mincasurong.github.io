About Minsu (Max) Chang, Ph.D.

I am a Staff Robotics Research Engineer (Ph.D.) at Samsung Electronics, SAIT, where I build multi-agent Task and Motion Planning (TAMP), Spatial Intelligence, and autonomous laboratory robotics systems. My current research pairs Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini Robotics-ER 2 with deterministic kinematic safety shields in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.0 and ROS 2 Jazzy, intercepting 610/610 injected hardware faults before actuation and cutting 3-arm VLA planning API cost by 76.6% to $0.01972 per 9-block assembly trial.

In 2026, I won 1st Place out of 1,500+ applicants and 111 finalist teams at the Google Gemini 3 Seoul Hackathon with GeminiSpace. Across ten years in robotics, I have published 21 peer-reviewed papers (IEEE ICRA, IEEE/RSJ IROS, IEEE TNSRE, BioRob, ICORR, JKROS), filed or registered 14 patents (10 US, 4 KR), and deployed the KFDA-certified SUBAR lower-limb exoskeleton across 6 clinical hospitals in Korea.
