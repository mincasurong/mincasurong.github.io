About M9G (Minsu Chang, Ph.D.)

I am a Roboticist and Physical AI Systems Architect (B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Sogang University) operating under the engineering identity M9G — From Machine to General Intelligence by Agent-enabled System Engineering (m incasuron g × m insu chan g). My work bridges deterministic nonlinear/adaptive robot control with frontier multimodal Vision-Language-Action (VLA) planning and multi-agent software harnesses.

From commercializing KFDA-certified lower-limb rehabilitation exoskeletons (SUBAR, deployed across 6 Korean hospitals) and authoring ICRA 2025 (OPPA) & IEEE TNSRE (AI Therapist) papers, to winning 1st Place at the Google Gemini 3 Seoul Hackathon 2026 (GeminiSpace) and architecting Project STAMP & m9g-agent-corps, I build closed-loop physical and agentic systems verified under real hardware-in-the-loop constraints.
