About Minsu (Max) Chang, Ph.D.

Robotics Research Engineer (Ph.D.) at Samsung Electronics, SAIT specializing in multi-agent orchestration for Spatial Intelligence, Physical AI, and robotic Task and Motion Planning (TAMP) with foundation models (Gemini Robotics-ER and Gemini 4 Argon).

1st-Place Winner at the Google Gemini 3 Seoul Hackathon 2026 (GeminiSpace), first author at IEEE ICRA 2025 (OPPA) and IEEE TNSRE (AI Therapist), and co-developer of the KFDA-certified lower-limb rehabilitation exoskeleton SUBAR deployed across 6 clinical hospitals.
