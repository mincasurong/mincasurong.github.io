Empirical Comparison of VLA Task Planners on a 3-Arm 9-Block Tower

We evaluate three multi-robot task-planning configurations on a 3-arm Franka Emika FR3 station in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.0 (240 Hz PhysX TGS) bridged over FastDDS unicast to ROS 2 Jazzy (50 Hz damped least-squares inverse kinematics). Operating strictly on 3D /tf coordinate feedback without RGB camera frames, a software-pipelined schedule generated by Gemini 4 Argon paired with a Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite step executor reduces per-trial API cost from $0.08437 to $0.01972 (-76.6%) and finishes in 10 turns instead of 13.

1. Experimental Findings

  1. Symbolic 3D /tf Grounding Without Camera Streams: All three stages ran with overhead RGB rendering disabled (0 image tokens). Each turn received 50 Hz 3D /tf block and end-effector coordinates, a deterministic spatial blueprint, and a pre-execution reachability and ownership check. All three planners stacked 9/9 blocks with zero collisions and a 100% verifier pass rate, avoiding Isaac Sim Replicator render overhead and per-turn base64 image token costs.
  2. Hybrid Brain-Executor Routing (Stage 3): Running Gemini 4 Argon once on Turn 0 (thinking_budget=256) to synthesize the 10-turn pipelined plan and delegating Turns 1–10 to Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (thinking_budget=0) reduced total API cost to $0.01972 (a 76.6% reduction vs. pure Argon at $0.08437 and 74.9% vs. ER-2 at $0.07860) while lowering mean step latency on Turns 1–2 from 13.31 s to 4.17 s.
  3. Software-Pipelined Scheduling (10 vs. 13 Turns): Gemini Robotics-ER 2 executed in wave-locked barriers: after picking 3 blocks on Turn 1, it placed Layers 1–3 serially (T2..T5), leaving FR3_1 and FR3_2 idle until Layer 3 completed. Gemini 4 Argon interleaved outer-table picks with central placements starting on Turn 3 (pick(FR3_1, B2) || place(FR3_2)), finishing in 10 turns and saving 10,929 input tokens (-22.6%).

3.1 Quadratic Input Token Growth & Cost Decomposition

With camera streams disabled, input tokens consist of the static system prompt and tool schemas (Bbase ≈ 2,800 tokens including the Turn-0 strategy) plus ΔBturn ≈ 250–380 tokens of tool-call and controller-response history appended per turn. Because the full conversation history is re-sent on each turn, cumulative input tokens grow quadratically with turn count T:

InputTokens(T) ≈ T · Bbase + [ T(T − 1) / 2 ] · ΔBturn

Reducing the schedule from 13 turns to 10 turns eliminates the three largest context turns at the tail of the episode (where each call exceeds 4,500 input tokens), saving 10,929 input tokens. Under per-token API pricing (gemini-4-argon at $2.00/1M input and $10.00/1M output; gemini-3.5-flash-lite at $0.30/1M input and $2.50/1M output), Stage 3 cost decomposes into:

CStage 3 = [ 2,800 × ($2.00 / 1M) + 220 × ($10.00 / 1M) ]Turn 0 Argon Brain ($0.00780) + [ 34,648 × ($0.30 / 1M) + 609 × ($2.50 / 1M) ]Turns 1–10 Flash-Lite ($0.01192) = $0.01972
